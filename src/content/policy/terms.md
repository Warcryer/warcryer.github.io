# Terms of use

These are the terms for the Warcryer service: the server that the released Windows app and its browser version connect to. The service is run by Warcryer ("we"). The app's source code is public under the MIT licence, and that licence, not this page, covers the code.

They are written to be read. If something here is unclear, ask on the [Warcryer Discord](https://discord.gg/qf4meEMfhp).

## It is a beta, and it is free

- Warcryer is in beta. Nothing is charged for it.
- It is still being tested. It may be slow, wrong or unavailable at times, and features may change or go.
- We may end the beta, or stop the service altogether. If we do, or if paid plans are introduced, we will say so on the Discord beforehand. Nobody will be charged without choosing a plan themselves.
- Do not make something that cannot fail depend on it.

## Your account

- You sign in with Discord, so you must be allowed to use Discord under its own terms.
- An account is for one person. What is done with your account is yours to answer for.
- You can delete your account, and what is kept about you, at any time in the app under **Settings → Account**. What is kept and for how long is set out in the [privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Alliances

- An alliance is started by one player, its owner, with a code from us during the beta. The owner and the admins they appoint run it: they decide who is in it and who leads its clans.
- What happens inside an alliance is first of all the business of the people who run it. If you have a problem with how your alliance is run, take it to them.
- An alliance has a plan, which limits how many approved members it may have. The limit only stops it growing; it never removes anyone.
- An owner can hand the alliance to another player or delete it. Deleting it removes its clans, events, attendance and log for good.

## What you may not do

- Break the law with it, or help anyone else to.
- Harass, threaten or abuse people, or use it to spread hatred of people for who they are.
- Share other people's private information.
- Record what others say without their knowing, where the law where you or they live requires their consent. The service itself does not record voice.
- Pretend to be someone else, or to speak for us.
- Attack or strain the service: flooding it, probing it for weaknesses without telling us what you find, getting round its limits, or using a changed app to hear or speak where your role does not allow.
- Sell or rent access to the service.

Warcryer is not connected with any game's publisher. Following your game's own rules is up to you.

## What you put in

Names, clan names and tags, crests, event names, notes and what you write in an operation's chat are yours. By putting them in you allow us to store them and show them to the members of your alliance, which is what the service is for (a chat line is shown only to the players on the net it was written on). Do not upload a crest or use a name that you have no right to use.

Voice is not recorded. The chat is text, and it is kept: for as long as your alliance's admins have chosen, and never longer than the operation it belongs to. Those who run an operation can remove a line and switch a player's writing off for that operation.

If an account or an alliance breaks these terms, or puts the service or its other users at risk, we may ban the account from the service, pause the alliance's events, or remove the alliance. Where we can, we will say why if you ask on the Discord.
