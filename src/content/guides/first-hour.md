# Your alliance's first hour

For the player who starts an alliance. Follow it top to bottom and by the end your clans' leaders are in, your members are arriving, and your first operation is planned. The other guides go deeper: [members](/guides/members/), [clan leaders and officers](/guides/clan-leaders/), [alliance admins and event leaders](/guides/alliance-admins/).

Warcryer is in beta and free. You need a **beta code** to start an alliance: ask for one on the [Warcryer Discord](https://discord.gg/qf4meEMfhp). The players who join you need no code.

## 1. Install and sign in (5 minutes)

1. Download the installer from the [Releases page](/download/) and run it. If Windows SmartScreen warns (the app is code-signed, but the signature is new), click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
2. Click **Sign in with Discord**, approve in your browser, and confirm your in-game name.
3. Go through the three setup steps: microphone and headset, the three-second test, and your push-to-talk key.

## 2. Start the alliance (2 minutes)

1. On the **Clan** tab, under **Or start your own**, type the alliance's name, pick your game and type your beta code. Click **Create alliance**.
2. You land on the **Alliance** tab as its owner. The line under the tabs asks which class you play: answer it.

## 3. Fit it to your game (5 minutes, or later)

On the **Alliance** tab, **Game setup → Change the setup**. The game you picked filled in classes, roles and party size. Check them, add your own **kinds of event** (your sieges and bosses), and under **Your words** say what your players call a clan, a party and an operation if the app's words are not yours. Save. You can come back to this at any time.

## 4. Create your clans and bring their leaders in (10 minutes)

An invite link is the only way into your alliance.

1. Under **Clans**, create each clan with a name and a short tag.
2. Appoint yourself leader of your own clan with the selector beside it.
3. For every other clan: click **Invite links** beside it, set **Uses** to 1, tick **Let people who use it straight in**, click **Create invite link**, then **Copy link** and send it to that clan's leader privately.
4. When a leader has used their link, appoint them with the selector beside their clan. From then on they run their clan and make their own links.

If someone else will help you run things, make them an admin: **Players → Make admin**.

## 5. Bring your members in (10 minutes, then it runs by itself)

Each clan's leader does this for their own clan, on their **Clan** tab:

1. **Create invite link**. For a link posted where many can see it, leave the box unticked: whoever uses it lands on the waiting list and you approve the names you recognise. Tick the box only for a link you hand to people you know.
2. Post it in the clan's own Discord channel with one line: "Install Warcryer, open this link, click Open in Warcryer."
3. Approve the names that appear under **Waiting for approval**.

Members land in their clan's lounge as soon as they are in. Tell them to answer the line that asks for their class: the roster board uses it.

## 6. Save your constant parties (10 minutes)

Groups that always play together are saved once. On the **Clan** tab, under **Constant parties**, name one and add its members in order; the first leads it. Each clan's leader and officers do this for their clan, and a constant party's own leader can keep it up to date.

## 7. Plan the first operation (10 minutes)

1. On the **Operations** tab, **New operation**: a name, a kind of event, a start time, the event leader, and the clans taking part.
2. On its board, click **Add all** to place every constant party at once. Drag the rest from Reserve, or leave them: Reserve has its own net.
3. Everyone in those clans is asked whether they are coming. Watch the count on the board, and what each party still needs.
4. Optional: on the **Alliance** tab, connect a Discord channel under **Discord announcements**, so players without the app open hear about it too. It also reminds them half an hour before the start, and can ping a role.

## 8. Before the first real fight

- Run a short test operation with a few people the day before: **Start operation**, have everyone press their keys, **End operation**.
- Ask the event leader and party leaders to bind their Broadcast and Command keys (the app asks when they first have those nets).
- Read **A checklist for event night** in the [alliance admins guide](/guides/alliance-admins/).

## If something does not work

First look at the [status page](/status/), which says whether the service itself is working; the app links to it under **Settings → Help**. Then ask on the [Warcryer Discord](https://discord.gg/qf4meEMfhp). Say what you did and what you saw; a screenshot helps.
