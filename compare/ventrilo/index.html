Warcryer vs Ventrilo

Ventrilo is the voice many guilds raided on, and it is still here: a current client for Windows, macOS and Linux, and servers hosted by its owner. It keeps to what it always was, a server with channels and a push-to-talk key. Warcryer is built around the event instead of the channel.

Checked against Ventrilo's own pages on 18 November 2026. Warcryer is in beta.

The short answer

Choose Ventrilo if

  • You want a plain voice server with channels, and nothing to learn.
  • Your players are on macOS or Linux.
  • You need more than 300 people, up to the 400 its paid plan holds.
  • Your guild has used it for years and its channel layout is second nature.

Choose Warcryer if

  • Your events have layers: an event leader, clan leaders, party leaders, parties.
  • Leaders need to hear command and their own party at once, and speak to each with a different key.
  • You move players between parties during a fight.
  • You want sign-ups, party building and attendance where the voice is.

What is different in practice

Nets instead of channels

On a Ventrilo server a player is in one channel, and can listen in on others with phantoms. In Warcryer every player is on their party, their clan and Broadcast at once, leaders on Command as well, and speaks to each with its own key.

The roster places people

Ventrilo leaves it to each player to find the right channel and to an admin to move them. In Warcryer the parties are built on a board from who signed up, and starting the operation puts everyone where they belong.

Orders are heard

While the event leader speaks on Broadcast, party and clan talk is lowered for everyone and comes back when the order ends. Nobody has to be told to be quiet.

The event around the voice

Ventrilo is voice. Warcryer also asks who is coming, shows what each party still needs, and records who attended and for how long.

Where Ventrilo is the better choice

Warcryer does one thing, and it is young. These are its limits today.

  • It is a Windows app. There is a browser version for a quick look, but no macOS, Linux or phone app.
  • It is a hosted service. The Windows app is open source; the server is not public and you cannot run your own.
  • It is in beta: an alliance is started with a code from the Warcryer Discord and has room for 300 approved members.
  • You sign in with a Discord account.
  • It is built for organised events. There is no video, screen sharing, file sharing or positional audio, and the text chat exists inside operations only.

Coming over from Ventrilo

  1. Keep your Ventrilo server for everyday talk if you like it. Warcryer is for the events.
  2. Start your alliance in the app and choose the starting lists for your game, or write your own.
  3. Create your clans and send each clan leader an invite link.
  4. Plan the first operation, build its parties on the board, and go live.

Questions

Is Ventrilo still being developed?
Yes. At the time of writing its site offers a version 5 client for Windows, macOS and Linux, and hosted servers.
Is Warcryer free?
It is free while it is in beta. An alliance is started with a code from the Warcryer Discord.
How many players can a Warcryer alliance have?
300 approved members during the beta. Ventrilo's paid plan holds 400 online at once, so for a larger group Ventrilo has more room today.
Does Warcryer run on macOS or Linux?
No. It is a Windows app, with a browser version for a quick look. Ventrilo has clients for all three.
